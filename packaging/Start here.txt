Spool for Windows x64

1. Right-click the ZIP, select Extract All, and open the extracted folder.
2. Double-click Open Spool.cmd. On first use Spool downloads and verifies
   yt-dlp and FFmpeg (about 53 MB total) from their GitHub releases.
3. Your browser opens Spool. Paste a YouTube link, click Fetch video, and
   choose MP4 video or MP3 audio. Your browser saves the finished file.

Keep the Spool console open during downloads. Close it to stop the app.
If your browser does not open, copy the local address printed in the console.

Use Check for updates at the bottom of Spool to check GitHub Releases.
Choose Install update if a newer version is available. Finish any downloads,
close the console and reopen Spool to start the updated version.

Each PC needs an internet connection. All media processing takes place there.
Spool uses public videos you own or have permission to download.
Some videos may be unavailable or restricted by YouTube.

Tools and temporary media are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SpoolDownloader.
Temporary copies expire after roughly an hour while Spool is running.
To remove Spool, close it and delete its extracted folder and the
SpoolDownloader data folder. Files saved by your browser are separate.

Share the original ZIP with other Windows x64 users. The Node.js runtime and
its third-party notices are in runtime. The media tools come from
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp and https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static.
